I will be joining the [European Centre for Algorithmic Transparency (ECAT) Research Workshop 2026](https://algorithmic-transparency.ec.europa.eu/events/ecat-research-workshop-2026-genai-and-future-platforms-and-search-engines-2026-11-25_en) in Seville next week!
I will present our extended abstract **Capturing Risk Pathways in Generative AI: Lessons from Recommender Systems and Children**, building on our recent [UMAP paper](https://dl.acm.org/doi/full/10.1145/3774935.3806148).

In this, we discuss how risk assessment in recommender systems may obscure risks for underrepresented and already marginalized groups such as children.
We build on our insights and discuss how these concerns are further amplified by the increasingly individualized nature of GenAI systems, and how we can extend our risk assessment methodology for recommender systems to these technologies. We highlight the tensions between existing approaches to risk assessment, as proposed under the EU's DSA, and the individualized characteristics of interactions with GenAI tools.
