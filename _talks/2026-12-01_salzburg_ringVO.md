Invited Talk for the public lecture series ['Gekommen um zu bleiben. KI in Kunst, Forschung und Gesellschaft'](https://w-k.sbg.ac.at/veranstaltung/whats-inside-the-toy-box-how-recommendation-algorithms-shape-what-children-see-online-robin-ungruh/).


Abstract
======
Children spend much of their time online, leading to growing concerns about the risks they encounter and the effects these experiences have on them. Current attention centers on the symptoms of interactions online, including risks to children’s well-being and distressing situations, and public debates frequently invoke broad concepts such as AI and social media, but what happens if we look a little deeper?

In this talk, we unpack one of the systems shaping children’s everyday online experiences: recommender systems. These algorithms affect which videos, posts, games are suggested to children, shaping what they see, and what they do next. We explore how recommender systems work, what role they play in children’s online lives, and where potential concerns arise. This talk aims to make the influence of recommender systems more understandable and transparent and to encourage a more nuanced and informed conversation about how digital environments children grow up in are shaped.
